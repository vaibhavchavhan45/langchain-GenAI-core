Under open skies and glowing lights
Cricket lives in days and nights
A game of hope a test of nerve
Where skill and patience both must serve

A pitch lies waiting worn and true
Marked by steps of many who
Have stood before with bat in hand
Dreams tied tight with every stand

The bowler runs with steady pace
Plans written clear upon his face
The ball released with perfect seam
A test of focus a test of dream

The batter waits alert and still
Between control and force of will
One clean strike the crowd awakes
One small error the silence breaks

Some shots flow like moving art
Some blocks are brave acts of heart
Each run earned each wicket fair
Moments shared in open air

The crowd responds with roar and song
In joy and pain they all belong
For cricket speaks a common tongue
To old and young to every one

When the match ends and lights go dim
The love remains the passion swims
In streets and fields both near and far
Cricket lives just as we are

Not just a sport of score and claim
But pride and spirit in the game
As long as bat meets ball in play
Cricket will never fade away
